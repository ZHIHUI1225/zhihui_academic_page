I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at [The Hong Kong Polytechnic University](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/), working in the [Romi Lab](https://www.romi-lab.org/) under the supervision of [Professor David Navarro-Alarcon](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/me/people/academic-teaching-staff/david-navarro-alarcon-prof/?sc_lang=en). My research interests include multi-agent systems, formation control, task and path planning, and deformable or non-prehensile object manipulation.

I was also a visiting student at the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia from October 2024 to March 2025.

My recent work focuses on cooperative robotic systems for transportation, curve tracking, and cargo manipulation. More details are available on the [Publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) and [CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}) pages.
