I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at [The Hong Kong Polytechnic University](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/), where I work in the Romi Lab under the supervision of Professor David Navarro-Alarcon. My research focuses on multi-agent systems, formation control, task planning, path planning, and the manipulation of deformable or non-prehensile objects.

Before joining PolyU, I received my M.S. in Control Science and Engineering from Harbin Institute of Technology, advised by Professor Chuanjiang Li and Professor Yanning Guo, and my B.Eng. in Automation from Beijing University of Chemical Technology. From October 2024 to March 2025, I was also a visiting student at the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia, working with Professor Lorenzo Sabattini.

My recent work studies how multiple robots can coordinate their motion and interaction to achieve transportation, curve tracking, and cargo manipulation tasks in structured and uncertain environments. I am especially interested in geometric and control-theoretic methods for cooperative robotic systems.

Selected recent publications include work in *IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters*, *IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems I/II*, *IEEE/ASME Transactions on Mechatronics*, *Optimal Control Applications and Methods*, and *Advances in Space Research*. A complete list is available on the [Publications](/publications/) page.

I was awarded the National Scholarship in 2021 and have contributed to research projects on multi-agent systems, non-prehensile robotic manipulation, and aerospace mission planning.

Please feel free to contact me by email if you are interested in collaboration on robotics, multi-agent coordination, or related control problems.
